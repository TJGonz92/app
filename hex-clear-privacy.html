Privacy

Updated 26 August 2026

Your scores, settings, and saved games live on your phone. Nothing is uploaded to a Hex Clear server. Uninstall the app and that data goes with it.

Ads come from Google AdMob. Google may use an advertising ID and basic device info to serve and measure ads. In places like the EU and UK, we ask before that happens. You can change this later in Android’s ad settings, or in Hex Clear → Settings → Ad settings when that option is shown.

Google’s own policy: policies.google.com/privacy

Hex Clear does not make you an account. It does not ask for your name, email, or location. There is no chat and no cloud save.

This game is not meant for children under 13.
