WreckerPay Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 3, 2026 · App ID: app.wreckerpay

WreckerPay (“the App”) is a personal job log and estimated-pay tool for company drivers. This policy describes how data is handled.

1. Data we store

WreckerPay stores the following only on your device:

  • Jobs you enter (dates, accounts, miles, hours, notes, pay snapshots)
  • Accounts and rate history you configure
  • App settings (pay rule, pay period, theme, work timer logs, tip counters)

There is no WreckerPay cloud account. We do not operate a backend that receives your jobs or accounts.

2. Data we do not collect

  • No automatic upload of job or account data to WreckerPay servers
  • No advertising ID tracking by WreckerPay
  • No sale of personal data

3. Export and backup

You may export a pay period (Excel) or create a full JSON backup. Those files leave the device only when you share them through the system share sheet (email, Drive, Files, etc.). WreckerPay does not transmit those files for you.

4. Purchases (Pro)

Optional Pro features may be sold through the app store (Google Play / Apple). Billing and purchase status are processed by the store, not by a WreckerPay payment server. Until in-app purchase is enabled, Pro may be unavailable or offered as “coming soon.”

5. Permissions

WreckerPay is designed to work without special location or contacts permissions. File access is limited to what the system share sheet and file picker provide when you export or import a backup.

6. Children

The App is intended for adult workers. It is not directed at children under 13.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the revised policy.
